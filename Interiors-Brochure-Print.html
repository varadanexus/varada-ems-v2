Representative Project

A home, reimagined.

The brief. A representative four-bedroom apartment engagement, beginning as a bare shell and envisioned as a warm, contemporary family home — open, light-filled, and clutter-free, with clever storage throughout.

The result. We opened up the living and dining, introduced a floating staircase and layered lighting, and worked in walnut veneer, marble, and handloom textiles for warmth. Every fitting — from the modular kitchen to the wardrobes — was custom-built and installed by our own team, and handed over move-in ready.

Good to Know

Frequently asked.

Q.How long does a project take?

A full home fit-out typically takes 10–14 weeks from design sign-off, depending on size and scope. We share a committed timeline before work begins.

Q.How is payment structured?

Payments are staged against milestones — design, execution, and handover — with clear, tailored estimates agreed upfront before any work begins.

Q.Do you handle everything, or just design?

Everything. Design, civil work, joinery, electrical, furnishing, and styling are delivered under one contract and one point of contact.

Q.Can I track progress?

Yes — every client gets secure portal access to view 3D designs, approve each stage, and follow progress and timelines from anywhere.

Q.Will you work with my architect?

Absolutely. We collaborate with your architect or work from your existing plans, and can also handle layout design ourselves.

Q.Is the work under warranty?

Yes. Our workmanship is warranty-backed, with responsive aftercare long after the keys are handed over.

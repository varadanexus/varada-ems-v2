# Government and Public-Authority Data Request Response Procedure

**Owner:** Privacy and Legal Function, Varada Nexus Private Limited  
**Approved by:** Management of Varada Nexus Private Limited  
**Effective date:** 29 July 2026  
**Review cycle:** At least annually and after any material legal, regulatory, or platform change  
**Applies to:** All Varada Nexus personnel, contractors, systems, EMS modules, Nexus Social, connected platforms, and service providers that may process personal data

## 1. Purpose

This procedure governs every request from a court, law-enforcement body, regulator, intelligence or national-security authority, government department, or other public authority for personal data processed by Varada Nexus Private Limited.

No employee or contractor may disclose personal data in response to such a request outside this procedure.

## 2. Intake and preservation

1. Forward the request immediately to the Privacy and Legal Function at `varadanexus@gmail.com`.
2. Preserve the original request, delivery envelope or headers, attachments, identity and contact details of the requester, and all subsequent correspondence.
3. Do not confirm that data exists, disclose data, or notify the affected person until the request has been assessed.
4. Apply a narrowly scoped legal hold only when preservation is legally required. A preservation hold does not itself authorise disclosure.

## 3. Legality review

Before any disclosure, the Privacy and Legal Function must document:

- the identity and authority of the requesting body and official;
- authenticity of the request using independently verified contact information;
- applicable law, jurisdiction, legal basis, warrant, order, summons, notice, or other authority relied upon;
- whether the request is binding on Varada Nexus;
- whether required signatures, approvals, dates, service requirements, and procedural safeguards are present;
- the persons, accounts, identifiers, data categories, and time period specifically covered;
- confidentiality or non-disclosure restrictions and whether they are legally valid;
- whether an emergency request satisfies the applicable emergency threshold; and
- whether external legal advice is required.

Informal, voluntary, ambiguous, defective, or unsupported requests must not result in disclosure.

## 4. Challenging unlawful or disproportionate requests

Varada Nexus will seek clarification, narrow, object to, or challenge a request when reasonably available grounds exist, including when the request:

- lacks valid legal authority or proper jurisdiction;
- is unauthenticated, procedurally defective, vague, or overbroad;
- requests data outside the stated investigation or lawful purpose;
- conflicts with applicable privacy, confidentiality, contractual, or fundamental-rights obligations;
- seeks data that Varada Nexus does not control or cannot lawfully obtain;
- prohibits notification without a valid legal basis or for an excessive period; or
- presents a material conflict of laws.

Where appropriate, the Privacy and Legal Function will request withdrawal or amendment, seek protective conditions, escalate to management, obtain external counsel, or pursue available administrative or judicial remedies. Disclosure is suspended while a challenge is pending unless applicable law requires otherwise.

## 5. Data minimisation

If disclosure is legally required, Varada Nexus will:

- disclose only the minimum data expressly covered by the valid request;
- apply the narrowest responsive account, person, field, event, and date range;
- exclude, redact, or segregate unrelated personal data, privileged material, credentials, secrets, and third-party information;
- avoid creating new derived datasets when existing responsive records are sufficient;
- use a secure transmission method and verify the recipient;
- record exactly what was disclosed; and
- retain disclosure copies only for the documented legal, audit, or defence period.

Bulk or unrestricted access to production systems is prohibited unless specifically required by valid law and approved by management after documented legal review.

## 6. Documentation and accountability

The Privacy and Legal Function must maintain a restricted request register containing:

- a unique request reference;
- receipt date, requesting authority, jurisdiction, official contact, and request type;
- affected service, accounts, data subjects, data categories, and date range;
- legal basis and legality-review outcome;
- challenges, narrowing discussions, legal advice, approvals, and deadlines;
- preservation, search, redaction, and minimisation steps;
- final decision, disclosure date, recipient, secure delivery method, and exact records disclosed;
- notification decision and any non-disclosure expiry date; and
- closure date, retention date, and post-request review.

Access to the register is limited to authorised management, privacy, legal, security, and audit personnel. Metrics may be reported in aggregate without compromising lawful restrictions.

## 7. Emergency requests

An asserted emergency does not bypass verification. Varada Nexus must independently verify the requester and document the imminent risk of death or serious physical harm, the legal basis, why ordinary process is inadequate, and the minimum data necessary to address the emergency. Management and the Privacy and Legal Function must approve any emergency disclosure when practicable.

## 8. Notice to affected persons

Varada Nexus will notify affected persons before disclosure when legally permitted and operationally safe. If notice is prohibited, the restriction and expiry must be documented and reviewed. Notice will be provided when the prohibition expires unless law continues to prevent it.

## 9. Service providers and cross-border requests

Requests concerning data held by a service provider must be routed through this procedure. Varada Nexus will not instruct a provider to disclose more data than Varada Nexus could lawfully disclose itself. Cross-border requests require conflict-of-laws and transfer-safeguard review before disclosure.

## 10. Training, audit, and non-retaliation

Personnel with access to personal data must be informed of this escalation requirement. Compliance with this procedure is reviewed at least annually and after every request. Good-faith escalation or objection to an unlawful disclosure must not result in retaliation.

## 11. Current transparency statement

Management confirmed on 29 July 2026 that Varada Nexus Private Limited did not provide Meta user personal data to public authorities for national-security purposes during the preceding 12 months.

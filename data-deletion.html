Privacy Request

User Data Deletion

Disconnect Meta accounts immediately or request deletion of data associated with EMS, Nexus Social, Facebook, Instagram, and other connected services.

Effective: 28 July 2026 Request channel: varadanexus@gmail.com

Option 1: Disconnect a connected account

  1. Sign in to Varada EMS.
  2. Open Nexus Social → Social Accounts.
  3. Select the connected Facebook, Instagram, Threads, or advertising account.
  4. Choose Disconnect and confirm.

This revokes the EMS connection and schedules stored access tokens and integration credentials for deletion. You may also revoke access directly in Meta Business Settings or Facebook’s Apps and Websites settings.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Email varadanexus@gmail.com with the subject User Data Deletion Request and include:

  • Your name and business email used with Varada EMS.
  • Your organisation or workspace name.
  • The connected Facebook Page, Instagram professional account, or advertising account name.
  • Whether you want one integration removed or the full EMS account deleted.
Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment card information, or government identity documents by email. We will request only the minimum information needed to verify authority over the account.

What we delete

After verification, we delete or anonymise applicable profile data, integration tokens, connected-account mappings, imported social content and metrics, messages or comments stored by EMS, campaign records stored by EMS, and other personal data not subject to mandatory retention.

Timing and confirmation

Verified requests are normally completed within 30 days. Complex requests, legal holds, security investigations, tax or accounting records, and backup rotation may require additional time. We will confirm completion or explain any data that must be retained and the reason.

Removing data from Meta

Deleting data from Varada EMS does not automatically delete original posts, messages, leads, campaigns, or other records held independently by Meta. Manage those records through the relevant Facebook, Instagram, Meta Business, or Ads Manager controls.

Questions

For assistance, contact varadanexus@gmail.com.

Good to Know

Frequently asked.

How long does a project take?

A full home fit-out typically takes 10-14 weeks from design sign-off, depending on size and scope. A committed timeline is shared before work begins.

Can I track progress?

Yes. Every client receives secure portal access to view 3D designs, approve each stage, and follow progress and timelines from anywhere.

How is payment structured?

Payments are staged against design, execution, and handover milestones, with clear tailored estimates agreed before work begins.

Will you work with my architect?

Absolutely. We collaborate with your architect or existing plans, and can also handle architecture and layout design ourselves.

Do you handle everything, or only design?

Everything: design, civil work, joinery, electrical, furnishing, styling, and handover under one contract and one point of contact.

Is the work under warranty?

Yes. Workmanship is warranty-backed, with responsive aftercare after the keys are handed over.
